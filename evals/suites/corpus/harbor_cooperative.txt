The Harbor Cooperative: a membership register

The Harbor Cooperative is a loose association of craftspeople, scientists and keepers who share tools, storage and a yearly assembly. This register records its members, what each of them does, and where each of them lives. Every entry stands on its own; no member works for more than one organisation, and no member has moved house since joining.

Anselm Marchetti works at Silverlake Brewing Company as the head brewer. Anselm Marchetti lives in Lisbon, where Anselm Marchetti paints watercolours of the harbour. Anselm Marchetti joined the Harbor Cooperative in 2007 and has attended almost every assembly since.
